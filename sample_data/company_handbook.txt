ACME Corp Employee Handbook

Welcome to ACME Corp. This handbook outlines our policies, culture, and expectations for all employees.

--- VACATION POLICY ---

Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid vacation per year. Vacation days accrue at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Unused vacation days may be carried over to the following year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Employees must submit vacation requests at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal. Vacation requests during peak periods (December 15 to January 5) require approval from both the direct manager and the department head.

--- REMOTE WORK POLICY ---

ACME Corp supports a hybrid work model. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. A reliable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps is required. Employees working remotely must be available during core hours of 10am to 3pm in their local time zone. All remote work arrangements must be approved by the employee's direct manager. Employees are expected to join video calls with their camera on unless previously agreed otherwise.

--- CODE OF CONDUCT ---

ACME Corp is committed to maintaining a respectful and inclusive workplace. Harassment, discrimination, and bullying of any kind will not be tolerated. Employees are expected to treat all colleagues, clients, and partners with professionalism and respect. Violations of the code of conduct should be reported to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline at ethics@acme.com.

--- HEALTH BENEFITS ---

All full-time employees are eligible for health insurance coverage starting on their first day of employment. ACME Corp covers 80% of the premium for individual coverage and 60% for family coverage. Dental and vision plans are available as optional add-ons. Employees have a 30-day window after their start date to enroll in benefits. Changes to benefit elections can be made during the annual open enrollment period held every November.

--- PERFORMANCE REVIEWS ---

Performance reviews are conducted twice per year: in June and in December. Employees are evaluated on their core competencies, goal achievement, and cultural contributions. Managers provide written feedback through the internal performance management system. Employees are encouraged to complete a self-assessment prior to each review cycle. Compensation adjustments, promotions, and development plans are discussed during the December review.

--- EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT ---

Employees may submit expense reports for pre-approved business expenses. Receipts are required for all expenses over $25. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Reimbursements are processed within two weeks of submission and paid via direct deposit. Expenses for meals during business travel are capped at $75 per day.
